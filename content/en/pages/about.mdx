# About Me

I'm Amr Madkour — a Senior .NET Engineer with over 8 years of experience building production systems that scale, perform, and last.

My work spans fintech, logistics, and enterprise SaaS. I've led platform migrations, designed event-driven microservices, and shipped products used by hundreds of thousands of users. I care deeply about code that is testable, observable, and built to change.

## What I Work With

My core stack is **.NET / C#** on the backend and **Next.js / TypeScript** on the frontend. I reach for Clean Architecture and Domain-Driven Design not as ceremony, but because they make teams faster over time by containing change and making intent explicit.

On the infrastructure side: **Azure**, **Docker**, **Kubernetes**, **GitHub Actions**. I've built OpenTelemetry observability pipelines, Redis caching strategies, and distributed systems designed to degrade gracefully under failure.

## How I Think About Engineering

Good engineering is not about using the most sophisticated tool — it's about choosing the right level of complexity for the problem at hand, making that choice visible, and leaving the codebase better than you found it.

I think about:

- **Seams** — where does this code need to change, and can I isolate that change?
- **Observability** — if this breaks at 3am, can on-call understand what happened?
- **Reversibility** — what decisions am I locking in, and are they worth locking in?
